OUR ROOTS

A movement shaped by service and fellowship.

CSI Madhya Kerala Diocesan Youth Movement traces its early annual youth gatherings to 1916. Known earlier as the Youth League, it adopted the name Youth Movement in 1978. That heritage of youth participation and service is the inspiration behind this celebration of talent.

Eligible individual points · group tie-break only

Kala Thilakam and Kala Prathibha are ranked by individual-event points from events with at least 3 competitors. Individual events with 1–2 competitors still earn full church points, but do not count toward these awards. Only when eligible individual points are tied, participating group members receive tie-break bonuses: 1st +5, 2nd +3, 3rd +1. Group-event points themselves go entirely to the church, never to individual accounts. Equal scores after the tie-break remain tied.
